1 · The role

Four parts of the job, and what I would do

CRM &
data quality

  • Turn the sales process into simple CRM fields and workflows
  • Weekly checks for missing, wrong or duplicate data
  • Each owner gets their own short list to fix

Pipeline &
forecast

  • Every opportunity has the right stage, value, date and next action
  • Fixed monthly forecast calendar for all regions
  • Follow up on missing or inconsistent numbers

Standards &
training

  • One set of data rules and definitions for all of EMEA
  • Short guides and CRM training per region
  • Find bottlenecks and suggest fixes

Reporting
& KPIs

  • Consolidate pipeline, shipments, revenue and forecast
  • Track KPIs, deadlines and action items
  • Tradeshow statistics and post-event analysis
2 · How I would work

A simple, fixed rhythm

Fixed dates mean less chasing. Regions know when input is due, and management knows when numbers are final.

Every week

Data and pipeline check

Send data quality lists to owners. Review stale deals and deals with past close dates. Update the action tracker.

Every month

Forecast cycle

Regions submit by day 3. I consolidate, check gaps and follow up by day 4. Management review on day 5.

Every quarter

Review and improve

Forecast accuracy, tradeshow results, process problems found, and the improvements planned for next quarter.
